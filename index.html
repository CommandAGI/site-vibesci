    What the twin catches

    01

    Carryover

    A tip that still holds the last solution leaves a film in the next well. On a blank, that film is the result.

    02

    A missed mix

    An unmixed well is a bolus sitting on diluent. The next aspirate takes the bolus, and the series walks away from its numbers.

    03

    A wrong volume

    Line 46 of the sample moves 20 µL where the series moves 10. The dilution factor is no longer the one on the page.

    04

    A target nothing meets

    A series can agree with itself and still miss the assay. Every well is held to its target, not to its neighbour.

    Every line it adds says why.
